Found in transit

Quiet, uncanny, unhurried. There are no monsters in the departure hall. Only the feeling that you should not be the one waiting.

No combat. No inventory.

You walk, you look, you carry, you throw, you wait. The building gives the orders.

Everything works

Every screen is lit. Every flight is on schedule. Every task is ordinary, and each one is slightly wrong.

The terminal moves

It only changes while you are not looking. Lights out, and the hall is different when they come back on.

You are not alone

Silhouettes sit and queue. Something broken is looking for you. It does not fight. It follows.

Inspirations

TERMINALS grew out of the uncanny corners of the internet and a few strange screens. Among them:

  • Kane Pixels and The Backrooms (Found Footage)
  • Severance
  • Twin Peaks
  • Crystal Castles, the band, and the look of their music videos
  • Denver International Airport and the lore around it
